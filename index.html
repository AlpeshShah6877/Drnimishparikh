Internal Policies & Procedures
Internal Policies & Procedures
For Addressing Conflict of Interest

SEBI REGISTERED RESEARCH ANALYST (Regulation 14 – SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014)

Objective

This document lays down the internal policies and procedures adopted by the SEBI Registered Research Analyst to identify, prevent, manage, and disclose conflicts of interest in compliance with Regulation 14 of the SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014. The objective is to ensure integrity, transparency, and investor protection.

Regulatory Framework

These policies are framed in accordance with:

  • SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014
  • SEBI Code of Conduct for Research Analysts
  • Relevant circulars and guidelines issued by SEBI from time to time
Definition of Conflict of Interest

Conflict of interest refers to any situation where the personal interest of the Research Analyst, or related parties, conflicts or appears to conflict with the interests of clients or the objectivity of research.

Identification of Conflicts of Interest

The Research Analyst identifies potential conflicts of interest including but not limited to:

  • Financial interest or ownership in securities being covered
  • Receipt of any compensation from the subject company
  • Personal trading in securities under research coverage
  • Relationship with directors, promoters, or management of subject companies
Personal Trading Policy

The Research Analyst follows a strict personal trading policy:

  • Personal trades are executed only after research dissemination
  • No front-running of client recommendations
  • All personal trades are documented and monitored
  • Trading in securities under active research coverage is restricted
Disclosure of Conflicts

All actual or potential conflicts of interest are fully disclosed in research reports and communications. Disclosures are made in a clear, prominent, and understandable manner to enable investors to take informed decisions.

Segregation of Activities

The Research Analyst maintains functional segregation between research activities and any marketing or commercial activities to ensure independence and objectivity of research.

Prohibition on Unfair Practices

The Research Analyst strictly prohibits:

  • Insider trading or misuse of unpublished price sensitive information (UPSI)
  • Manipulative or deceptive practices
  • Issuance of misleading or biased research reports
Record Keeping & Monitoring

Records relating to research reports, disclosures, personal trades, and conflict management are maintained for a minimum period of five years as prescribed under SEBI regulations. Periodic internal reviews are conducted.

Investor Grievance Redressal

Investor complaints, if any, are addressed promptly and in a fair manner. A proper mechanism is maintained for recording and resolving investor grievances.

Review & Amendment

This policy is reviewed periodically and updated as required to remain compliant with regulatory changes and best practices.

Declaration

This internal policy on conflict of interest has been adopted by the Research Analyst in compliance with Regulation 14 of SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014.
